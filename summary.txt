World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026.

Russia and Ukraine concluded US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi, agreeing on a major prisoner exchange without a ceasefire. Envoy Jared Kushner called it the war's largest release (Al Jazeera, Reuters, The National). Russia also struck six electricity substations in central Ukraine and a market in Odesa.

COP31 opened in Antalya, Turkey; UN chief Guterres urged a climate justice pact and fossil fuel taxes. UNFCCC, AP, and Manila Times note 102 leaders are attending, though US, Chinese, and Indian heads of state are absent.

Spain's first general strike in 14 years drew four million workers on 11 November over housing costs and wage stagnation (CNN, Reuters, CNA). Unions report rents now consume nearly all of a young person's monthly salary.

The DRC government reports 11,042 Ebola cases and 5,387 deaths, a public health emergency currently affecting eight provinces (Reuters).

The US Justice Department indicted three Iranian-backed operatives for alleged plots in Europe and the US, including at RAF Fairford (BBC, NYT). Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent was relocated due to death threats.

Israel's new parliament was sworn in without a new government, while strikes in Gaza killed eight, including a child (Zark News, Afterlight).

Russia's US ambassador, Alexander Darchiev, died in Moscow aged 66; the Russian Foreign Ministry and Reuters reported a sudden death, later linked to chronic illness.

Democrat Scott Colom defeated Republican Cindy Hyde-Smith in the Mississippi Senate race (AP).

The US House shelved the impeachment resolution against Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth, and Republicans named Jim Jordan as minority leader (CNN, Reuters).

Nine people were seriously injured in a bus crash in Seongnam, South Korea (Yonhap).
